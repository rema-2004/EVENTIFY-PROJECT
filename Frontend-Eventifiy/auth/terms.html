Terms of service

The rules for using EVENTIFY, for students and for the organizations publishing on it.

Last updated 4 September 2026

Who can use EVENTIFY

Anyone aged 16 or over can create a student account. Organization accounts are created by someone authorized to represent that organization, and are usable only after our review team verifies it.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself and your profile accurate. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Applications submitted from your account are treated as submitted by you.

What we expect from students

  • Represent your own skills and history honestly. Fabricated profiles get suspended.
  • Do not submit someone else's work, CV or certificates as your own.
  • Do not scrape, resell or republish listings from the platform.

What we expect from organizations

  • Publish only opportunities that exist, with accurate dates, requirements and prizes.
  • Use applicant data solely to assess that application.
  • Charging students to apply is not permitted, and neither is asking for payment details on EVENTIFY.

Review and removal

Every opportunity is reviewed before it goes live. We can decline or remove a listing that is incomplete, misleading or unsafe, and we can suspend an account that breaks these terms. Where the situation allows it, we tell you why and how to appeal.

Content you post

You keep ownership of everything you upload. You give us permission to display it on EVENTIFY for the purpose you uploaded it for — a profile shown to organizations you applied to, or a listing shown to students.

What EVENTIFY does not guarantee

We connect students and organizations. We do not run the events themselves and cannot guarantee an outcome, a prize, a placement or the conduct of an organizer. Match scores are a ranking aid, not a promise.

Changes

If these terms change materially, we notify account holders before the change takes effect. Continuing to use EVENTIFY after that date means the new terms apply.

Contact

Questions about these terms go to hello@eventify.io.
