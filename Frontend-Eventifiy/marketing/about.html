About EVENTIFY

Connecting ambition with opportunity.

EVENTIFY is an AI-matched platform that helps students and graduates discover hackathons, competitions, internships, courses and events built for them — and helps organizations reach the right people.

Our story

Built by people who were once on the other side of the application

EVENTIFY started from a simple frustration: great opportunities were scattered across dozens of pages, group chats, and outdated PDFs, and the students who needed them most were the least likely to find them in time.

We set out to build a single place where opportunities find you, powered by an AI companion — Rafeeq — that learns your skills and goals instead of asking you to sift through irrelevant listings.

Today EVENTIFY supports students, graduates, and the organizations running events, from independent student clubs to established institutions.

What we stand for

The values behind every feature

These principles guide how we design the platform, from how Rafeeq recommends opportunities to how organizers manage applicants.

Relevance over volume

We'd rather show you three opportunities that fit than thirty that don't. Matching quality comes first.

Fair access

A strong opportunity should be findable by any student who qualifies for it, not just those already in the right group chat.

Trust and verification

Organizations on EVENTIFY go through a verification step, so applicants know who they're actually applying to.
