About EVENTIFY

Opportunity should not depend on who you know

EVENTIFY started because the best competitions, workshops and internships were buried in group chats. We put them in one place and match them to what each student can actually do.

What we stand for

Three commitments behind every feature

They decide what we build, and just as often what we refuse to build.

Access before reputation

A first-year student and a final-year student see the same opportunities. Matching is based on skills, not on which club you joined.

Transparent matching

Every match score explains itself. If you scored 62%, you can see which requirement pulled it down and what to learn next.

Verified, or not published

Organizations are reviewed before they can post. No expired listings, no dead links, no opportunities that quietly never existed.

Our story

Built by students who kept missing deadlines

EVENTIFY began as a graduation project in Amman, after its founders missed a hackathon deadline that had only ever been announced in a group chat they were not in.

The first version was a shared spreadsheet of every competition the team could find. It spread across three universities in one semester — which made the real problem obvious: the opportunities existed, but nobody could see the ones that fit them.

Today EVENTIFY brings competitions, workshops, courses and internships into one verified feed, and Rafeeq — our AI companion — ranks them against the profile you build once.
