Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it — in plain language.

Last updated 4 September 2026

What we collect

Two kinds of data. First, what you give us: your name, email, university or organization, the skills on your profile, the files you attach to an application, and the messages you send us. Second, what your use of the platform produces: which opportunities you open, save and apply to, and the match scores generated from your profile.

Why we collect it

  • To match you with opportunities. Without your skills and level, a match score is meaningless.
  • To pass your application to the organization you applied to. They see the profile and files you submitted, nothing else.
  • To notify you before a deadline closes on something you saved.
  • To review organizations before they can publish, which is how listings stay real.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not pass your profile to organizations you have not applied to. We do not use the content of your applications to train models.

Who can see your profile

Your public profile is visible to other EVENTIFY users and to verified organizations. Your email, phone number and attached files are shared only with an organization you have applied to, and only after you submit that application.

Keeping data

Application records stay for as long as your account is open, so you can see your own history. Delete your account and the profile, files and application history are removed within 30 days. Aggregate counts that cannot identify you — such as how many people applied to an event — remain with the organization.

Your controls

  • Edit or remove anything on your profile at any time from your settings.
  • Request a copy of your data, or its deletion, by writing to hello@eventify.io.
  • Turn deadline and status notifications off without closing your account.

Cookies

We use one functional cookie to keep you signed in and a local preference for your light or dark theme. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set.

Questions

Write to hello@eventify.io or use the contact form. We answer within one working day.
